How to enjoy

Not sure what to do with a tin? Start here.

2 minutes

On toast

  1. Toast a slice of sourdough.
  2. Add sardines and a drizzle of their olive oil.
  3. Finish with lemon, black pepper and parsley.
5 minutes

In a salad

  1. Toss greens, cherry tomatoes and white beans.
  2. Flake in mackerel with lemon.
  3. Use the oil from the tin as dressing.
10 minutes

On a snack board

  1. Open two or three different tins.
  2. Add crackers, olives, pickles and cheese.
  3. Serve with a crisp white wine or sparkling water.

Our story

From the Portuguese coast to your pantry

Portugal has been canning fish since the 1800s. In towns along the Atlantic coast, sardines and mackerel are still cleaned and packed by hand, then sealed with good olive oil.

Mar Lusa means “Portuguese sea”. Our tins bring that tradition to your kitchen, with a design you'll want to keep on the counter.

Conservas is the Portuguese word for preserved fish. In Portugal, a tin of good conservas is a gift, a quick lunch or the start of a party.

Our tins are inspired by azulejos, the painted blue-and-white tiles you see on buildings all over Portugal.

FAQ & shipping

Good to know

Is the fish ready to eat?

Yes. It's fully cooked in the tin. Eat it straight away, cold or warmed up.

How long does a tin keep?

Unopened, about 5 years. Check the “Best by” date on the bottom (for example, Best by 12/31/2029). Once opened, keep it in the fridge and eat within 2 days.

How much is shipping?

Free on orders over $50. Otherwise $5.95. We ship from our US warehouse and your order arrives in 3–5 business days, with tracking by email.

How can I pay?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Is it sustainable?

We work with small Portuguese canneries that buy from local fishing boats. Each product page explains where the fish comes from.
